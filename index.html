Quality Steps

HOW WE PROCESS

Clean, hygienic, step-by-step value addition.

1

Maharashtra Sourcing

Raw dried tamarind selected from top agricultural harvest belts in Maharashtra.

2

Shelling & Curing

Outer shell removal, sun-curing, and husk cleaning at Dharmapuri unit.

3

Deseeding & Defibering

Meticulous removal of seeds, fibers, and veins to ensure pure seedless pulp.

4

Pressing & Packaging

Pressed into cakes (250g/500g/1kg), round balls, or wholesale 50kg PP bags.
